I am a PhD Student on a full scholarship at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU). My research is conducted at the Visual Computing section of DTU Compute, where I am privileged to be supervised by Prof. Morten Rieger Hannemose, Prof. Anders Nymark Christensen, and Prof. Anders Bjorholm Dahl.

I am currently conducting a research visit at the University of Hong Kong (HKU) in Prof. Yu Lequan’s lab, where I work on Agentic AI and AI agents.

I also serve as a Part-time Research Scientist at Retinalyze, bridging the gap between academic innovation and clinical application.

Previously, I earned my Master’s degree in Computer Engineering on a full scholarship at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME) and served as a Visiting Student at the Neural Information Processing Group (NIPG) at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE).

Research

My research focuses on Agentic AI, Generative AI, and Medical Image Analysis, leveraging intelligent AI systems and deep learning to solve challenging biomedical problems. My recent work explores:

  • Agentic AI for Medical Image Analysis: Developing intelligent agents that integrate multimodal reasoning, planning, and tool use to support automated medical image interpretation and analysis workflows.
  • Generative AI: Advancing medical text-to-image generation and visual counterfactual explanations at CVPR 2026 and DGM4MICCAI 2025.
  • Medical Image Segmentation: Developing robust Transformer and CNN architectures for clinical analysis, presented at ISBI 2026, ICONIP 2023, ICASSP 2021, and ICPR 2020.
